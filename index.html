Highway to Hill

Drop two pins on Canberra. Watch Dijkstra flood the city while Contraction Hierarchies thread a handful of shortcuts — same route, a fraction of the work.

Two routing algorithms race across Canberra's real road network to find the exact same shortest route — one searches almost everywhere, the other barely looks. Drag either pin to a new spot, switch on extra algorithms, and watch the difference for yourself. Every number here is measured live in your browser, never invented.
