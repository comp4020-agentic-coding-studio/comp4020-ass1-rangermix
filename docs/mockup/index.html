Page 2 /how/ — five chapters, one toy each

A vertical article. Desktop shows a chapter-progress rail; phone is plain flow. Every toy runs the same algorithm code as the big race, on a 12-node mini-town, and shares one control convention: ▶ play · step · ⟲ reset. Chapters 2–4 are mocked interactively below; 1 and 5 are described.

  1. What Dijkstra actually does

    30-second refresher: press play, the frontier expands ring by ring until B settles. Dijkstra is perfect and blind — it cannot know a node is irrelevant. (Toy: mini-town flood with a settled counter.)

  2. Contraction: remove a node without lying about distances

    The invariant: after deleting a node, every remaining distance is unchanged — shortcuts are the price, witnesses the discount.

    E routes four neighbour pairs. Before contracting, CH asks: for each pair, is through E the only shortest way?

    real version: tap ANY node to contract it; a disclosure animates the local witness Dijkstra; shortcut counter accumulates

  3. Order is everything

    Contract the same town in three orders; count the shortcuts each forces. Node selection is the knob that decides preprocessing quality.

  4. The hierarchy, revealed

    The money shot, on the real map: sweep from every road to only the top-ranked nodes. Suburbia fades; the arterials and bridges remain — CH discovered Canberra's road hierarchy from structure alone.

    notice: the bridge survives to the very top — bottleneck nodes rank high.

  5. The query: only ever climb

    Mini-town redrawn with height = contraction rank. Two searches climb — forward from A, backward from B — meet near the top, and the winning path unzips: shortcuts expand recursively back into real streets. Every shortest path has a summit; that's why 214 can beat 21,480.

    MVP ships this as a played-through animation; free A/B picking is the target tier.

User journey

0:00 · land

Read without waiting

Headline + core-interaction sentence render before the graph loads; skeleton map + progress bar behind them.

0:05 · wow

The race runs once

Flood vs sparks, counters tick, scoreboard locks: "same route, 99% less work — measured just now".

0:30 · poke

Make it personal

Drag pins home → uni. The flood covers your suburb; the sparks don't. Try to break it: presets, surprise me.

2:00 · learn

Five chapters

Refresher → contraction → ordering (lose to the heuristic once) → hierarchy revealed on the real map → the climb.

8:00 · prove

Back to the race

Chapter 5 echoes your own numbers; return, switch on A*, watch even the clever ones lose. Say the idea in one sentence.

Marker's 60-second skim: land → wow → scan chapter headings → footer credits. The whole idea is legible with zero operation, at both viewports.

Exit ramps: stop after the hero and you still got the idea; after ch. 2, the intuition; after ch. 5, enough to implement it.

Type

Display: heavy, tight — the exhibit voice

Instrument: ui-monospace — 21,480 settled · 38.2 ms · every number the site shows

Body: system sans at 1.55 line-height for chapter prose. No webfonts — the payload budget belongs to the map.

Motion rules

One thing animates at a time · every replay is ~2.5 s with a step/scrub alternative · prefers-reduced-motion swaps all animation for final states with the same numbers.
